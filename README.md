# Cards Against Our Ulibarri Family

A Cards Against Humanity–style party game for the family. Everyone plays on their own phone. The host creates a room and the others join with a 4-letter code or an invite link. Best with 7+ players (3 minimum, 20 max).

Play it: https://hulibarri.github.io/Card-against-/

- **Packs:** Main, Spicy 🌶️, Mormon, and Family (Taylor & baby Jo Jo). The host can turn packs on or off in the lobby.
- Baby Jo Jo cards are never paired with spicy cards.
- Blank cards let you write your own answers.
- Add your own cards by editing `cards.js`. Add one line per card.

Rooms connect phone to phone using PeerJS (`peerjs.min.js`, v1.5.4, MIT). The host's phone runs the game, so keep it open while you play.
